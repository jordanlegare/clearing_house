# CRA T3010 source

The plugin ingests the Canada Revenue Agency **List of charities** open-data publication derived from T3010 Registered Charity Information Returns.

Default dataset:

- Year: 2024
- Open Government dataset id: `80c00cdb-1358-415c-bb8b-0de7f12675b8`
- Publisher: Canada Revenue Agency
- Update frequency: annual

The importer does not assume the resource URLs remain stable. It calls the public Open Government Canada CKAN `package_show` endpoint, classifies the CSV resources by metadata/name, and then streams the selected resources.

Known dataset ids are also included for 2022 and 2023. Future years can be selected with `--dataset-id` or `OPEN_CANADA_T3010_DATASET_ID` without a code change.

## Data-quality rule

The source reflects information submitted by charities. A T3010 row is evidence from a public filing, not a guarantee of current registration, legal grant eligibility, service quality, or community impact. The matching layer must retain that distinction.
